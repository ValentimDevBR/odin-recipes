Easy Ground Beef Tacos

Description

Quick and delicious ground beef tacos seasoned with homemade spices. Perfect for busy weeknight dinners!

Serve with your favorite toppings like shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, cheese, and sour cream.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 8 taco shells (hard or soft)
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 diced tomato

Steps

  1. In a skillet over medium-high heat, cook ground beef until browned. Drain excess fat.
  2. Add taco seasoning and water. Bring to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes until sauce thickens.
  3. Warm taco shells according to package instructions.
  4. Spoon meat mixture into taco shells and add cheese, lettuce, tomato, and favorite toppings.
